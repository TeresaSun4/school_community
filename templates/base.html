FAQ / Help

FAQ & Help

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top navigation. You'll need to provide a username (at least 4 characters), email address, and password.

Can I change my username?

Yes! Go to your profile page and click "Edit Profile". Your new username must be unique and at least 4 characters long.

Posts & Content

What are the character limits?

Posts can be up to 5,000 characters long, while comments are limited to 1,000 characters. You'll see a character counter when writing.

Can I add images to posts?

Yes! You can upload one image per post. Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF. Max size: 2MB. Make sure you have rights to use the image.

Can I edit or delete my posts?

You can delete your own posts, but editing is not available. When you delete a post, all comments and likes are also removed.

Images & Copyright

What are the image upload rules?

You are fully responsible for all images you upload. Images must be created by you or used with clear permission. Don't upload internet images without legal rights.

Technical Support

The website isn't working properly

Try refreshing the page first. If problems persist, clear your browser cache or try a different browser. Contact support if issues continue.
