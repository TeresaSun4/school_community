Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new account?

Click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the page and fill in your username, email, and password. Your password must contain at least 8 characters, including uppercase, lowercase letters, and numbers. Once registered, you can post, comment, and personalize your profile.

How do I change my profile picture?

After logging in, visit your profile page and click the camera icon below your current avatar. You can upload an image in PNG, JPG, JPEG, or GIF format as your profile picture. The system will automatically resize the image and apply the changes.

How do I delete my account?

After logging in, go to your profile page and click on the "Settings" tab. At the bottom of the page, you'll find the "Delete Account" section. Click the "Delete My Account" button, and the system will ask you to confirm this action. Please note that account deletion is permanent, and all your data (including posts, comments, and personal information) will be completely deleted and cannot be recovered.

How does the platform protect my privacy?

We take user privacy very seriously. Your personal information is strictly protected and will not be disclosed to third parties without authorization. We use encryption technology to protect your password and sensitive data. You can control which information is visible in your profile settings at any time. Detailed privacy policy can be viewed in the "Privacy Policy" link at the bottom of the page.
