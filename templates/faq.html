Help & Questions

Find answers to common questions about our website

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top navigation. You'll need to provide a username (at least 4 characters), email address, and password. Make sure to use a valid email address as you may need it for account recovery.

Can I change my username after registration?

Yes! You can change your username by going to your profile page and clicking the "Edit Profile" button. Your new username must be unique and at least 4 characters long.

How do I reset my password?

Currently, password reset functionality is not available. If you forget your password, please contact the site administrator. We recommend keeping your password secure and memorable.

Posts & Content

What are the character limits for posts and comments?

Posts can be up to 5,000 characters long, while comments are limited to 1,000 characters. You'll see a character counter when writing that will warn you as you approach these limits.

Can I add images to my posts?

Yes! You can upload one image per post. Supported formats are PNG, JPG, JPEG, and GIF. Images must be under 2MB in size. Make sure you have the right to use any images you upload.

What categories can I choose from for my posts?

Available categories include: Academic, Social, Events, Help, General, and Māori. Choose the category that best fits your post content to help other users find relevant discussions.

Can I edit or delete my posts after publishing?

You can delete your own posts, but editing is not currently available. When you delete a post, all associated comments and likes will also be removed. Think carefully before posting!

Images & Media

What are the rules about uploading images?

You are fully responsible for all images you upload. Images must be either created by you or used with clear permission from the original owner. Do not upload pictures from the internet unless you have legal rights to use them. Copyright violations may result in content removal.

Can I upload a profile picture?

Yes! You can upload a profile picture from your profile page. The same image responsibility rules apply - make sure you have the right to use the image you choose as your avatar.

Community Guidelines

What content is not allowed on the platform?

Prohibited content includes: false or misleading information, content that infringes on others' rights, harmful code/viruses, harassment or threats, and any content that violates applicable laws. Please be respectful and constructive in all interactions.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Currently, there's no built-in reporting system. If you encounter inappropriate content, please contact the site administrator directly. We reserve the right to review and remove content that violates our community guidelines.

Technical Support

The website isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try refreshing the page first. If problems persist, try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser. For ongoing technical issues, please contact the site administrator with details about the problem you're experiencing.

Is my data safe and private?

We take your privacy seriously. Please review our Privacy Policy for detailed information about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. Your posts and comments are visible to other users of the platform.
