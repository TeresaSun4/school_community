Community Rules

To maintain a healthy and positive community environment, all users must follow these rules

Core Principles

Mutual Respect

Respect all community members regardless of their background, opinions, or identity. Discrimination, harassment, or insulting behavior will not be tolerated.

Honesty and Integrity

Maintain honesty and do not post false information or misleading content. Respect the intellectual property of others and do not infringe on copyrights.

Constructive Communication

Maintain a constructive attitude when participating in discussions, and remain civil and polite even when disagreements arise.

Prohibited Behaviors

Harmful Content

Posting violent, pornographic, discriminatory, hateful, or other content that may cause harm to others is prohibited.

Spam

Posting spam advertisements, repetitive content, or irrelevant content is prohibited.

Illegal Content

Posting any content that violates local laws and regulations is prohibited.

Sensitive Information

Do not post personal private information of others, including but not limited to phone numbers, addresses, emails, etc.

Multi-Account Abuse

Creating multiple accounts for malicious activities such as manipulating likes or spamming comments is prohibited.

Content Guidelines

Relevance

Content posted should be relevant to the selected category, ensuring information is accurate and valuable.

Clear Expression

Try to use clear and concise language to express your meaning, avoiding excessive jargon or technical terms.

Appropriate Titles

Use titles that accurately describe content, avoiding all-caps or excessive punctuation.

Violation Handling

Progressive Handling

Violations will be handled according to severity, ranging from warnings to permanent access bans.

Appeal Mechanism

If you believe you have been wrongfully penalized, you can appeal by contacting the administrators.

Rule Updates

Community rules may be updated at any time, and users are responsible for regularly checking the latest rules.
